# Outreach targets + ready-to-send drafts

Researched Jul 2026 (links checked at that date; verify before sending).
Everything here is pre-written so an outreach action = pick one, personalise
one line, send. PITCH.md is the attachment/paste for all of them.

## Podcasts (pitch a segment, not the site)

| Target | Why them | The angle |
|---|---|---|
| **HUNGRY** (challenger FMCG founders pod) | Big challenger-founder audience, loves contrarian operators | "Margin is the insurance on a bad forecast" — the 48p working, live |
| **The Friday Accelerator** (Karen Green, ex-Tesco/Boots buyer) | Host is a former buyer — the Dictionary is her love language | "What buyers say vs what they mean — and the maths underneath" |
| **The FMCG Podcast** (UK, sales/marketing careers) | NAM-heavy listenership | "The two numbers every NAM confuses: their 35% and yours" |
| **The FMCG Guys** (European CPG) | Broader reach, fee/marketplace topics land | "What Amazon actually takes from a £1.50 product" |

Pitch email (podcasts):

> Subject: a guest who brings a calculator
>
> I'm a UK grocery commercial director. I built getgross.co.uk — free
> calculators that do the maths challenger brands walk into range reviews
> without: what the retailer really makes, what a promo actually costs,
> what margin survives a bad forecast.
>
> I can bring three worked examples your listeners can re-run with their own
> numbers while listening, e.g.: the 48p margin that survives a halved
> forecast; the buyer's "two more points" that's actually five; the healthy
> listing that still needs £40k of cash. Numbers first, war stories second.
>
> One-pager attached. No product to sell — everything's free.

>

> If your readers would use it, it's linkable as a reference. And if a data
> piece helps ("what promos actually cost in 2026", worked openly), I'll
> build it with the workings shown.
