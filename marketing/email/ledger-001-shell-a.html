THE WORKING
THE LISTING, DECLARED
RSP (zero-rated)£2.50
Stores300
COGS per unit£0.90
Launch trade investment£15,000
Forecast rate of sale4 units/store/wk
The forecast says 4. Forecasts are opinions.

The most important question. What ROS does this listing NEED to survive?

Say the honest downside is half. ROS 2.
Fixed money per week£15,000 ÷ 52 = £288
Downside units per week2 × 300 = 600
Margin that breaks even£288 ÷ 600 = 48p
Under 48p a unit, a 50% forecast miss ends this listing.

Now walk 48p into the meeting:
48p margin on 90p COGS = cost price£1.38
£1.38 into £2.50 leaves the retailer44.8%
The buyer asking 45% instead of 44.8% sounds like rounding. It's the difference between surviving the miss and not.

If surviving isn't the ambition — if the listing must still MAKE £5,000 in the bad year:
Downside units, full year2 × 300 × 52 = 31,200
Margin required(£15,000 + £5,000) ÷ 31,200 = 64.1p
Cost price£1.54
Retailer's share, maximum38.4%
The same 64.1p that merely survives the bad year makes £25,000 if the forecast holds. Insurance that pays out in both states of the world.
