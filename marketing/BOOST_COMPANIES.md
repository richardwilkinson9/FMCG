## What to do with it (playbook §3)

1. Page invites: spend credits on Tier 1 and Tier 2 people first —
   they've already seen a card and paused.
2. Till crew: Lily Vanilli and the Tier 2 founders are candidates —
   real people in the real audience, already primed.
3. Commenting beat: add the Tier 2 brands' pages to the daily 15-minute
   beat list; comment with numbers, never links.
4. No re-boost until an organic winner exists (see the paid re-entry
   gate: best organic engagement rate, £10/day × 14, tappable
   destination, kill-rules from day one).
