# The LinkedIn playbook — brand-page-only edition

Constraint accepted: no personal-profile promotion. The plan shifts from
"borrow the founder's network" to "the brand does the talking; compounding
channels carry more weight". Slower (months 2–6, not weeks 2–6), fully
workable. Written Jul 2026.

## 1. The anonymous insider — make the mystery the brand

Anonymity is a feature: it explains the candour, protects it, and makes
people curious. Rules: never confirm or deny authorship, to anyone; nothing
traceable (no real meetings, no category+retailer+timing combos); the persona
is the SITE, not a character.

**Page About section (paste):**
> GROSS. is free commercial calculators for UK FMCG brand teams — retailer
> margins, gross-to-net, trade spend, cash, Amazon, TikTok. Built and
> maintained by a serving UK grocery commercial director who has sat on both
> sides of the table and would like to keep sitting there. The maths is
> checked. The fees are dated and sourced. The rat is not real. getgross.co.uk

**Pinned post (the origin story, minus the identity):**

Also: one line on the Ledger page + pitch kit — "Written by a serving UK

## 2. The page posts — the format does the walking

Pages get ~10–20% of personal-post distribution. Beat it with hand-to-hand
shareability, not volume. The reach plan is other people's networks.

**Hooks (first two lines are 80% of the post):**
- setup-punchline: "your buyer:" → image lands it
- uncomfortable number: "48p. that's what stands between this listing and a bad year doing real damage."
- flat accusation: "your bestseller might lose money. most people have never checked."
- confession bait: "every NAM has heard this sentence. most have said it."
- correction invite: "this maths is either right or the site gets fixed. tell us which."

**Every post ends with an implicit question** — "what's your buyer's
favourite?", "which one did you hear this week?", "wrong? show your working."
Inviting attacks on the maths is the brand's best engagement mechanic: the
page replying with visible arithmetic is the advert.

**Monthly document post** — LinkedIn PDFs get 2–3× dwell; use the existing
carousel pipeline ("10 translations from the buyer's dictionary" / the Ledger
issue as slides). **Monthly poll** — four Dictionary options; the answers are
next month's content.

**Cadence:** Tue + Thu 07:45–08:30 UK, plus one Saturday morning. Never two
in a day. First 60–90 minutes decide everything → see §3.

## 3. Seed engagement — the invisible hand

Comments count ~5–10× a like; >5-word comments count more; page replies count
again. Ten early comments with replies can 20× a page post's reach.

- **The till crew:** 5–10 trusted industry mates in a WhatsApp group. Deal:
  link 3×/week; if it makes you smirk, comment something real; never say you
  know the owner. Real people in the real audience, primed to be first.
- **Personal-account risk ladder** (choose the rung): DMs (invisible — the
  "first 200" play survives fully; script: "not mine officially, but this is
  very us — [link]") → page invites (~250 credits/month, sender's name in
  small print; use all, every month) → reactions (nobody audits likes) →
  comments (visible; owner's call) → reposts (public promotion; the line).
- **Page replies to every comment within the hour.** Hostile comments are
  the best comments; show the working.

## 4. Commenting AS the page

Pages can comment anywhere; almost no brand does it well. 15 min/day.

**Beat list:** The Grocer + Retail Week grocery posts (pricing, promos,
supplier terms, fee changes) · the UK grocery commentariat (ex-buyer/GSCOP
voices, retail analysts — pick five, ring the bell) · challenger founders
posting listing wins · category managers posting charts.

**Formula:** never congratulate, never agree emptily, never link. One number
or one translation per comment. Examples:
- On "we're launching in 400 stores": "congratulations. now the maths: 400
  stores at 3/store/wk needs ~X cases at the DC by week 2 — and the shelf-fill
  invoice lands before the first payment does."
- On a promo-pressure article: "reminder that a supplier-funded 25% off
  doesn't touch the retailer's margin. GSV × 25%, off your line, every week
  it runs. 'investment', as it's known."
- On a margin chart: "worth saying which base — retailer margin is % of
  shelf, brand margin is % of net revenue. same word, two rulers."

Comment on non-posting days too. No links unless asked. "What's GROSS.?"
in replies = win.

## 5. The compounding channels

- **SEO:** GSC verification + sitemap is the gate. Guides target real query
  families; from week 6–8 use GSC's query report to choose the next four
  guides from data. Nothing for 8–12 weeks, then likely the biggest channel.
- **Email:** export gate + nudge + Ledger page. Add "forward this to the NAM
  who needs it" to the shell footer. Targets: 100 subs by end of month one,
  45%+ opens, reply personally to every reply.
- **Communities:** YF community (free) — join as yourself, low-key, answer
  margin questions with worked numbers; a helpful member sharing a link reads
  differently from a brand post. **Bread & Jam 14–15 Jul 2026 (Business
  Design Centre, Islington)** — go. Listening play (Ledger/Dictionary fuel)
  + stunt play: the Dictionary printed as physical till-receipt cards, left
  on tables — other people photograph and post them, which is the whole model
  in one object.
- **PR:** the persona is the story. Subject line for The Grocer: "the rate
  card nobody would put their name to". Then KamCity/NamNews. One pitch a
  fortnight.

## 6. Paid — small, late, on proof

Nothing for 2–3 weeks. Then: boost the best card by engagement RATE, £10/day
× 14 days. Targeting: UK · Food & Bev / Consumer Goods · NAM, Commercial
Manager/Director, Category Manager, Founder (≈50–150k audience). Objective:
engagement (buying followers + social proof; £2–4/follower acceptable, CTR
>0.6% = creative works cold). Insight Tag: at ~300 site visitors, build the
matched audience and retarget calculator users with one ad — "The Ledger.
Monthly. No selling." Kill rules: CTR <0.3% after £50 → kill creative;
>£6/follower → kill audience; never boost an organic flop.

## The week, assembled

- **Sun (30 min):** queue Tue/Thu/Sat from CALENDAR + CAPTIONS; brief the crew.
- **Daily (15 min):** 3 page comments from the beat list; reply to everything
  on our posts within the hour.
- **Mon (5 min):** automated check-in + The Till; watch followers-per-week
  until week 4, then add sessions.
- **Monthly:** 250 invites · document post · poll · Ledger issue · analytics
  screenshot into the build session.

**Working = by week 4:** followers growing >50/week unpaid, and one post
past 2,000 impressions. If neither: fix the first hour (seeding), not the
cards.
