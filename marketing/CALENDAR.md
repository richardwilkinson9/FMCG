the channel; reshare from the GROSS. page an hour later.

## Standing rules

- Miss a slot? Skip it. Never post two on one day to catch up.
- Every post that mentions a number must survive scrutiny — the maths is on
  the site, so hostile commenters check. That is the point; welcome them.
- Comments are content: the best replies become next month's Dictionary
  entries and Ledger questions.
- Monthly: Ledger issue (write → send → publish on-site → NUMBER card the
  same day). The issue is the anchor; the week's other posts orbit it.
