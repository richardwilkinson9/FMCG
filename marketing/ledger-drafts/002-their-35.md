THE LEDGER — No. 002

## Title

THEIR 35% ISN'T YOUR 35%

## Standfirst

Same word. Two different numbers.

## THE NUMBER

**5.1%**

Source line: What "two more points" of retailer margin actually costs the
brand margin in the worked example below. Not two.

## THE WORKING (draft copy)

THE SHELF, DECLARED

    RSP (20% VAT)                                £1.50
    Shelf price ex-VAT                           £1.25
    Retailer margin                                35%
    COGS per unit                                £0.32

Their 35% is measured off the shelf: £1.25 × 35% = 43.75p.
They pay you the rest: £0.8125. That's your invoice price.

Your margin is measured off what you bank:

    You bank                                   £0.8125
    less COGS                                   −£0.32
    Gross margin                               £0.4925
    As % of what you bank                        60.6%

Two people at one table. One says 35, one says 60.6.
Both correct. Different bases.

Now the buyer asks for "just two more points":

    Two points, their base            £1.25 × 2% = 2.5p
    Your margin drops           £0.4925 → £0.4675
    That's                           5.1% of your margin

Their two is your five. The bases do that, every time,
in one direction only — theirs.
